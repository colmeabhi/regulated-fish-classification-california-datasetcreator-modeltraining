# Can I keep this fish?

Upload a photo of a fish caught off California. A two-stage ResNet50 model predicts its regulatory category (prohibited, restricted or legal) and species, and the app says whether you can keep it.

Source: https://github.com/colmeabhi/regulated-fish-classification-california-datasetcreator-modeltraining

Not legal advice. Confirm with the [CDFW ocean regulations](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations).
